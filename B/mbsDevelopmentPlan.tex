\section{Risks and Lifecycle Gates}
Unselected hardware and transport may invalidate detailed design; unresolved timing and resource limits prevent meaningful performance acceptance; an undefined platform matrix prevents compatibility claims. Owners and affected decisions are identified in \hyperref[Table:OwnedQuestions]{the question register}. No probabilities, severity ratings, schedules, or mitigations have been approved.

The documentation gate requires supported metadata, explicit gaps, consistent traceability, a complete build, visual inspection, and human review. Later needs, requirements, architecture, implementation, verification, validation, and software-release gates require their own evidence and authorized dispositions.
